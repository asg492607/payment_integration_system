Why PayForge?

🤖

Auto-Verification via SMS

Your Android phone forwards bank credit SMS to our server. We parse the amount, match the order, and activate service — no customer input needed.

🔥

Firebase Real-time Updates

Your dashboard and apps receive instant payment notifications via Firebase Realtime Database — the moment money hits your account.

🛡️

Replay Attack Protection

Each UPI reference number is stored and checked. The same SMS can never activate two orders — mathematically impossible to game.

🏢

Multi-tenant Enterprise

Each startup gets their own UPI config, webhook secrets, and isolated data. Host once, serve unlimited startup teams.

💰

Zero Platform Fees

Unlike Razorpay (2%) or Stripe, PayForge charges ₹0 per transaction. You receive 100% of what the customer pays.

📱

Android SMS Forwarder

Install a free SMS Forwarder app on your phone, configure the webhook URL and secret — done in under 5 minutes.
