📧 Bank Email Alert Polling

✅ Most Indian banks (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak) send a transaction email for every credit. ASG Payment Gateway polls this inbox every 2 minutes and verifies payments automatically — no phone needed.
⚠️ Important: Create a dedicated Gmail account just for bank alerts (e.g., payments@gmail.com). DO NOT use your personal Gmail — the server will read it.

Step 1 — Register email with your bank

HDFC: NetBanking → Customer Information → Email Alerts → Credit Alert → Enable
SBI: YONO App → Services → Manage Alerts → Credit → Enter email
ICICI: iMobile → Account → Register for Email Alerts → Transactions
Axis: NetBanking → My Profile → Email Alert Settings → Credit Transactions
Kotak: Net Banking → Profile → Manage Alerts → Credit Transactions

Step 2 — Generate Gmail App Password

1. Enable 2-Step Verification on Gmail
2. Go to: myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
3. Select app: Mail → Generate
4. Copy the 16-character password (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop)
